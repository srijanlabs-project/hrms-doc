# 01. Brand Identity

## 1. Brand Position

`Staffsy` is an enterprise HRMS brand with an AI-enabled but human-centered tone.

The product should feel:

- dependable
- warm but not casual
- efficient
- modern
- operationally trustworthy
- globally deployable

The product should not feel:

- playful consumer-tech
- futuristic to the point of ambiguity
- overly corporate and cold
- decorative at the cost of readability

## 2. Brand Promise

Core brand message:

- one people platform for operations, decisions, and employee experience

Supporting themes:

- human centered
- AI powered
- enterprise grade
- scalable
- accessible

## 3. Visual Personality

Primary visual attributes:

- clean white work surfaces
- deep teal as the system anchor
- warm orange as the action and guidance accent
- restrained blue, green, yellow, and red semantics
- rounded corners that feel friendly but still enterprise-ready
- soft shadows instead of heavy elevation
- clear section framing for dense business content

## 4. Logo Usage

Logo construction observed from the approved direction:

- icon plus wordmark
- icon uses teal and orange
- wordmark uses deep teal

Logo rules:

- default full-color logo on white or very light neutral backgrounds
- reverse logo only on dark teal navigation or hero surfaces
- never place the logo on orange, red, or high-noise image backgrounds
- preserve clear space equal to the icon height around the lockup
- minimum digital width:
  `96px` for lockup
- minimum icon-only width:
  `20px`

## 5. Brand Voice in UI

Microcopy should be:

- clear
- polite
- direct
- informative
- action-oriented

Examples:

- use `Review payroll exceptions`
- do not use `Uh-oh, something went wrong`
- use `Approval pending with Finance`
- do not use `Waiting...`

## 6. Brand Motion

Motion should communicate:

- system response
- workflow progress
- AI processing state
- navigation continuity

Motion rules:

- keep transitions between `120ms` and `220ms`
- avoid bounce, overshoot, or playful elastic movement
- use fade and slight translation for drawers, toasts, overlays, and step transitions
- loading should appear controlled and lightweight

## 7. Brand Imagery

Preferred imagery style:

- illustrated or lightly stylized HR and workplace scenes
- clean iconographic support illustrations
- neutral, inclusive, professional photography where needed

Avoid:

- stock-photo overload
- dark dramatic imagery
- neon, cyber, or abstract AI visual clichés

## 8. Brand Application by Persona

Employee-facing surfaces:

- slightly softer spacing
- more positive empty states
- faster action discovery

Manager and HR-facing surfaces:

- higher density
- stronger task prioritization
- more visible workflow state

Org admin and platform surfaces:

- maximum clarity
- stronger system-state indicators
- less decorative treatment
