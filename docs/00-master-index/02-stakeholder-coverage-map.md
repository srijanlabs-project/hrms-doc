# 1. Purpose

This document maps the major stakeholder groups for the Enterprise HRMS application to the documentation perspectives they require. The objective is to ensure the documentation library is not written only for one audience, such as business or engineering, but serves every critical participant in the product lifecycle.

# 2. Primary Stakeholder Groups

- Business sponsors
- Product managers
- HR operations teams
- Talent acquisition teams
- Payroll and compensation teams
- Finance approvers and controllers
- Compliance and audit teams
- Employees
- Managers
- Leadership and executive users
- UX and product designers
- Frontend engineers
- Backend engineers
- Data and analytics teams
- QA teams
- DevOps and platform teams
- Implementation and migration teams
- Support and service teams

# 4. Coverage Rule

Every major document should explicitly answer at least one of the following:

- What problem does this solve for the business?
- How does the end user interact with it?
- How does the system behave?
- How is it secured, audited, and controlled?
- How is it tested, supported, and implemented?

If a document does not materially help any stakeholder group make a decision or perform a task, it should be refined or removed.
