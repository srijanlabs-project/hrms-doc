# 1. Purpose and Scope

PF governs employee provident fund eligibility, contribution calculation, employer obligations, challan generation, filing support, and audit traceability for applicable employees.

In scope:

- PF eligibility and enrollment
- Wage basis and contribution computation
- Voluntary and policy-driven overrides
- UAN, transfer, exit, and compliance file support
- Reconciliation, remittance, and audit controls

# 2. Business

Provident Fund compliance is a statutory and employee-trust requirement. Failures in contribution accuracy or filing discipline create legal exposure, penalties, employee dissatisfaction, and payroll rework.

Business outcomes:

- Ensure accurate statutory contribution processing
- Maintain complete member records and filing readiness
- Support payroll-compliance reconciliation and audit defense
- Reduce manual intervention for recurring statutory operations

# 3. Functional

The system shall support:

- Eligibility determination based on legal entity, location, wage thresholds, joining date, and worker type
- PF enrollment capture including UAN, previous membership, transfer details, and declaration forms
- Contribution calculation using statutory wage rules and organization policy for wage basis
- Employer and employee share calculation, EPS diversion rules where applicable, and rounding configuration
- Voluntary provident fund support where policy allows
- Joiner, leaver, international worker, and exempted establishment scenarios
- Generation of PF registers, remittance data, and filing extracts
- Reconciliation between payroll results, statutory payable, and remittance outcomes
- Arrear and retro pay PF impact calculation

Validation rules:

- PF contribution shall use effective statutory configuration applicable to payroll period and legal entity
- Manual override of contribution basis shall require authorized reason capture
- UAN duplication checks shall run before final enrollment approval
- Exit date updates after payroll finalization shall trigger recalculation review
- UAN shall be validated as exactly `12` numeric digits before enrollment approval
- PF enrollment shall fail if required identity, employment, or legal-entity prerequisites are missing

# 4. UX

The user experience shall provide:

- HR operations form for PF enrollment and exception handling
- Payroll review screen showing PF wage basis, employee share, employer share, and variance
- Reconciliation workspace comparing payroll totals, challan totals, and payment confirmation
- Employee self-service view for PF identifiers and contribution history where allowed

# 5. API

Representative APIs:

- `POST /api/v1/compliance/pf/enrollments`
- `PATCH /api/v1/compliance/pf/enrollments/{enrollmentId}`
- `GET /api/v1/compliance/pf/contributions`
- `POST /api/v1/compliance/pf/remittance-batches`
- `POST /api/v1/compliance/pf/reconciliation`

API requirements:

- Calculation APIs shall expose wage basis and rule version used
- Remittance APIs shall support reruns without duplicate batch creation
- Integration responses shall preserve statutory reference numbers

# 6. Database

Core entities:

- `pf_enrollment`
- `pf_contribution_result`
- `pf_rule_version`
- `pf_remittance_batch`
- `pf_reconciliation_record`
- `pf_exit_record`

Key data requirements:

- Enrollment records shall store UAN, membership status, effective dates, and exemption attributes
- Contribution results shall capture payroll period, wage basis, employee share, employer share, EPS split, and adjustment amount
- Remittance batches shall store status, challan references, totals, and payment evidence

# 7. Events

The platform shall publish:

- `pf.enrollment.created`
- `pf.contribution.calculated`
- `pf.remittance.generated`
- `pf.remittance.reconciled`
- `pf.member.exited`

# 8. Reports

Required reports:

- PF contribution register
- PF exception and override report
- UAN missing or invalid member report
- Payroll versus remittance reconciliation report
- PF arrears impact report

# 9. Dashboards

Dashboards shall show:

- PF-covered employee count
- Period contribution payable and remitted status
- Enrollment defects and missing master data
- Filing or payment delays by payroll period

# 10. Security

Security controls shall include:

- Restricted access to statutory identifiers and sensitive personal data
- Controlled override rights for wage basis and compliance status
- Encryption for exported files and payment references
- Download restrictions for bulk compliance artifacts

# 11. Audit

The audit trail shall capture:

- Eligibility changes and enrollment updates
- Manual contribution overrides
- Remittance batch generation and cancellation
- Rule-version changes and retroactive recomputation

# 12. AI

AI capabilities may include:

- Detection of anomalous PF variances or eligibility mismatches
- Forecast of contribution spikes due to retro payroll
- Suggested cleanup of incomplete enrollment data

AI guardrails:

- AI shall never alter statutory outcomes without human approval
- Compliance calculations shall remain rules-engine driven, not probabilistic

# 13. Test Cases

Minimum test coverage shall include:

- Eligible employee receives correct contribution split
- Manual override requires authorization and reason
- Retro payroll updates PF payable correctly
- Duplicate UAN is blocked or flagged per policy
- Remittance rerun does not duplicate payable records

# 14. Workflows

Primary workflow:

1. Employee eligibility is evaluated.
2. Enrollment data is captured and validated.
3. Payroll computes PF contribution each period.
4. Remittance batch and compliance artifacts are generated.
5. Reconciliation and closure complete the period.

# 15. State Machine

Supported states:

- `not-applicable`
- `pending-enrollment`
- `active`
- `exception-review`
- `remittance-pending`
- `remitted`
- `exited`

# 16. Permissions

Permissions shall include:

- Manage PF enrollment
- View and override contribution details
- Generate remittance batches
- Approve statutory exceptions
- Access reconciliation and audit reports

# 17. Notifications

Notifications shall support:

- Missing enrollment data alerts
- Payroll variance warnings
- Remittance due-date reminders
- Reconciliation failure or mismatch alerts

# 18. Configuration

Administrators shall configure:

- Statutory thresholds and effective-date versions
- Wage components included in PF basis
- Employer policy on wage capping and voluntary contribution
- Export formats, due dates, and approval controls
- UAN format, duplication, and exception-handling policy

# 19. Edge Cases

The design shall address:

- Employee crosses wage threshold after joining
- Backdated joining or exit changes prior-period contribution
- Exempted establishments with different processing needs
- International worker handling
- Multiple payroll runs in one month with combined statutory liability
