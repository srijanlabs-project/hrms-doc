# 1. Purpose

This pack adapts the platform for IT services, product engineering, consulting, BPO, KPO, shared services, and technology-enabled operations where project alignment, access lifecycle, skills, hybrid work, and utilization are central.

# 2. Industry Workforce Profile

Typical workforce segments:

- software engineers
- QA engineers
- support analysts
- project and program managers
- consultants
- customer success teams
- contact-center or operations staff
- product teams
- shared services teams
- contractors and global mobility assignees

Common operating conditions:

- project and account-driven organization structures
- hybrid or remote work patterns
- strong laptop, access, and asset dependency
- billability, utilization, and skills visibility needs
- international mobility and distributed teams

# 4. Preconfigured Operating Model

The pack should seed:

- legal entity, country, office, practice, account, delivery unit, and project hierarchy
- worker categories for permanent, contract, trainee, intern, project-based, and support roles
- hybrid work and attendance policy templates
- skill taxonomy and certification masters
- career tracks for technology, consulting, support, and management streams
- project assignment and bench-management workflows

# 5. Functional Specialization

People and mobility:

- onboarding with asset, access, and project assignment dependencies
- joiner, mover, and leaver workflows integrated with identity and asset controls
- mobility and relocation tracking for cross-location deployment
- bench to project assignment and release management

Recruitment and capability:

- requisitions aligned to skill, experience, and project demand
- resume, skill, and interview evaluation templates
- internal mobility and project-change workflows
- learning paths tied to certification and promotion readiness

Performance and experience:

- goal and competency cycles aligned to projects and delivery units
- multi-rater or project-feedback support
- recognition, engagement, helpdesk, and employee communication integration

Payroll and rewards:

- variable pay tied to performance or utilization where configured
- shift and allowance support for support or operations teams
- mobility or deputation allowances for international assignments as needed

# 6. Security, Privacy, and Audit Controls

IT or ITES-specific controls:

- access lifecycle must closely track employment and project movement
- privileged or client-facing access should have additional approval chains
- exited employees must lose authentication and endpoint trust quickly
- support sessions should never bypass customer-confidentiality rules
- device, asset, and identity evidence should align with JML audit trails

# 7. Integrations and Data Exchange

Common integrations:

- identity providers and device-management tools
- project and ticketing systems
- LMS and certification platforms
- payroll and finance systems
- collaboration and communication tools
- background verification and mobility vendors

# 8. Reports, Dashboards, and AI

Priority reports:

- utilization and billability report
- bench aging and deployment readiness report
- certification and skills gap report
- attrition and retention risk report
- joiner, mover, leaver SLA report
- asset and access compliance report

Priority dashboards:

- CHRO workforce dashboard
- delivery leadership dashboard
- people manager dashboard
- talent and skills dashboard
- HR operations control tower

AI use cases:

- skill inference and career-path suggestions
- attrition and bench-risk summarization
- job description and interview guide generation
- policy assistant for hybrid work, mobility, and leave questions
- manager summary of team performance and wellbeing signals

# 9. UX and Persona Expectations

UX should emphasize:

- polished desktop experiences with strong mobile ESS support
- project, practice, and manager-aware dashboards
- self-service around profile, skills, certifications, helpdesk, and documents
- quick manager actions for approval, assignment, and performance inputs
- terminology that works across product, services, and operations environments

# 10. Implementation Pack Assets

The pack should ship with:

- practice, project, and account hierarchy templates
- hybrid work policy templates
- skill taxonomy and certification masters
- JML workflow presets
- performance and project-feedback templates
- utilization and talent dashboard presets
- AI copilot prompt bundles

# 11. Risks and Edge Cases

Critical edge conditions:

- worker assigned to multiple billable and non-billable projects simultaneously
- project release before access and asset cleanup complete
- remote onboarding with delayed hardware delivery
- timesheet and attendance policies colliding for support shifts
- global assignment affecting payroll, leave, and access rules together

# 12. Exit Criteria

IT or ITES pack implementation is acceptable when:

- project, skill, and access lifecycle flows are configured
- hybrid workforce and JML controls are validated
- utilization, talent, and compliance dashboards are trusted
- employee and manager journeys support real delivery operations
